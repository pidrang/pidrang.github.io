Privacy

What this site collects, why, and how to have it removed. Last updated 2026-10-21.

Page views

Pages of pidrang.dev count visits with GoatCounter, a hosted, open-source analytics service. It records the page visited, the referring site, the browser, the operating system, the screen size and the country. It sets no cookies and does not store IP addresses, so nothing ties a visit to a person. The numbers are used only to see which pages are read.

The documentation and the live demos are not counted.

Newsletter

  • What is collected: your email address, when you sign up with the form on the home page.
  • Why: to send Pidrang release notes and articles, about once a month. Nothing else, and the address is never shared or sold.
  • Who handles it: Buttondown stores the list and sends the emails on behalf of the project.
  • Consent: after signing up you receive a confirmation email; you are subscribed only once you confirm it.
  • Unsubscribe: every email has an unsubscribe link that works in one click.
  • Deletion: write to contact@pidrang.dev to have your address removed from the list.
